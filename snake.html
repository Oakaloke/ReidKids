How to play +
  1. Pick a color and pace, then press Let’s play.
  2. Use arrow keys or WASD. On a phone, swipe the board or tap the arrows.
  3. Each fruit makes you one segment longer. Avoid the edges and your own tail. You can’t turn straight backward.
  4. Press Space, P, or Pause for a break. Your game also pauses when you leave this tab.

Your best for each pace stays on this device when browser storage is available.
