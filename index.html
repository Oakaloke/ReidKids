About Reid Kids

Stories that point to Jesus

Reid Kids is a Christian children's brand creating coloring books, storybooks, and family resources that plant seeds of faith and fill childhood with wonder.

We believe every child deserves to know they are loved, known, and made with purpose — and that families grow stronger when they grow in God's love together.
