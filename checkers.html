Official checkers rules: if a jump is available, you must take it.

How to play
  • Move diagonally forward, one dark square at a time.
  • Jump over the other color to capture. In Standard Rules, you must take an available jump.
  • In both rule sets, once you choose a jump, keep jumping with the same piece while another capture is possible.
  • Reach the far edge to earn a crown. Kings move and jump forward or backward. Earning a crown ends your turn.
  • Win by capturing all opposing pieces or leaving them with no moves.
  • Three repeated positions, or 40 moves each without a capture or an uncrowned-piece move, make a draw.
