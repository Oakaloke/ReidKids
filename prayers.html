A prayer for our family

Our Father, who art in heaven,
hallowed be thy Name,
thy kingdom come,
thy will be done,
on earth as it is in heaven.

Give us this day our daily bread.

And forgive us our trespasses,
as we forgive those
who trespass against us.

And lead us not into temptation,
but deliver us from evil.

For thine is the kingdom,
and the power, and the glory,
for ever and ever. Amen.

Pray it with your family.
Any time, any place.

A little story behind the prayer

Words shared through generations

Nearly 2,000 years ago, in the first century, Jesus taught his friends how to pray. We can read his teaching in Matthew 6 and Luke 11. Today, families around the world still share this prayer, asking God for daily care, forgiveness, and help.

In 1549, Thomas Cranmer helped bring together the first Book of Common Prayer, so people in England could worship together in English rather than Latin. A later edition appeared in 1662, and the prayer book tradition became part of Christian worship around the world.

The traditional words on this page appear in the 1979 American Book of Common Prayer, Rite One. Many of these familiar words are much older than that edition. When you pray them with your family, you are joining generations of people who have turned to God in love and trust.
