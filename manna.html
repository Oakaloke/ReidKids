An arcade adventure inspired by Exodus 16.
How to play +
  1. Move with ← → or A / D, your mouse, or a finger on the board. The buttons below work, too.
  2. Press Let’s play to launch. Bounce the ball off your basket to clear the golden manna pieces.
  3. Each piece is 10 points. Clear all 3 mornings to finish. A new morning restores one chance, up to your starting total.
  4. Missed the ball? Take a breath, then press Try this bounce. Your remaining pieces stay cleared.
  5. Space launches or pauses. P pauses, and Escape takes a break. Leaving this tab pauses the game.

Changing pace starts a new game after you confirm. This is a visual game, with keyboard controls and spoken status updates.
