# ReidKids Mascot — Image Prompt

Explorer hero + eagle sidekick ("Liberty") for the ReidKids brand.
Indiana-Jones-style adventurer with faith details (ichthys hatband, cross
compass north point, pocket Bible, dove charm on Liberty's leg band).

## Positive prompt

Realistic photo of an original adventure-explorer character for a family-friendly
brand called ReidKids. A confident, friendly man in his early 30s with a warm smile,
short sandy-brown hair, light stubble, bright adventurous eyes. He wears a sun-faded
brown felt fedora with a leather band bearing a tiny stamped ichthys fish, a rust-red
bandana, a cream henley shirt with rolled sleeves under a worn brown leather bomber
jacket, and a shoulder holster. A brass compass on a leather cord with a small
engraved cross at the north point hangs at his chest. A coiled bullwhip clipped at
his hip; sturdy dark trousers and worn brown boots. A canvas satchel across his body
holds a rolled treasure map and a small worn leather pocket Bible with a red ribbon
bookmark.

A majestic bald eagle sidekick named Liberty — expressive eyes, proud playful
personality, white head, golden beak, rich brown feathers, wearing a tiny leather
leg band with a small gold dove charm.

Pose: mid-action, sprinting across a crumbling canyon rope bridge, revolver drawn,
hat brim low, bandana flapping, Liberty soaring just ahead with talons out leading
the way. Background: deep canyon gorge, dust and pebbles falling, dramatic
golden-hour light, sun rays streaming down from above. Warm color palette (sunset
orange, turquoise, canyon red, sky blue), appealing proportions, polished, high
detail, dynamic composition.

blurry, watermark
